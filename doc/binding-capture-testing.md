# Physical binding capture

1. In Decktation's main panel, select **Change binding**.
2. Release all buttons, then hold the desired combination on one controller.
3. Release the combination. Check that it is saved and displayed.
4. Close the QAM and verify that holding the new binding records and releasing it transcribes normally.
5. Confirm capture never starts a recording or confirms/cancels a pending transcription.
6. During capture, touch Cancel or wait 20 seconds. The previous binding must remain unchanged.
7. Disconnect the controller while holding a candidate combination. The previous binding must remain unchanged.
8. Repeat with another supported controller. Buttons from different controllers must not be combined.

Steam and Quick Access buttons are excluded. Capture accepts one to five supported buttons. Recording remains hold-to-record, as in master.

The previous press-to-bind implementation was tested physically on a Steam Deck. This revision on the current master requires fresh physical validation.
